# Daftar Bacaan: AI Data Centers

## Dampak lingkungan umum (AS)
- Guidi et al. (2024). Environmental burden of US data centers in the AI era. arXiv:2411.09786. CO2 dan listrik; data scraping, Baxtel, OpenStreetMap.
- Kollar & Grady (2025). Data centers and climate: a spatial analysis of US data centers. Environ. Res. Commun. 7(11), 111005. Suhu, air, emisi; datacenters.com, Argonne, EPA, WRI Aqueduct.
- Bolanos-Zuniga & Lamadrid (2026). Environmental and economic implications of AI data centers in the US. Listrik, cooling, backup; evaluasi.
- Xiao et al. (2025). Net-zero pathways for sustainable AI servers in the USA. Nature Sustainability 8(12). Bacaan tambahan.

## Satelit dan data center
- Yang, Zhou & Niu (2026). Spatial distribution and environmental attributes dataset of China's large-scale data centers in 2024. Scientific Data. Sentinel-2 + Random Forest, spatial probability surface. Repo: https://github.com/ziweiyang1206/data-center-classification (belum dibaca lengkap). Referensi untuk kesesuaian lokasi di Indonesia.
- Chakraborty (2024). Towards a comprehensive assessment of AI's environmental impact. arXiv:2405.14004. Satelit + data energi untuk mengisi gap data.
- Molla (2026). Digital heat islands and environmental justice of AI infrastructure in Phoenix. ECOSTRESS LST, NDVI Landsat, survei.
- **Marinoni et al. (2026). The data heat island effect. arXiv:2603.20897. PAPER ACUAN UTAMA.** MODIS LST 500 m, 2004-2024; rata-rata kenaikan LST sekitar 2,07 C; pengaruh hingga sekitar 10 km; sekitar 343 juta penduduk terdampak.
